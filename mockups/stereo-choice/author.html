Stereo question

After correct arrows the learner draws the product's stereochemistry by tapping the highlighted centres. The step's product (as drawn in Mechanism) is the right answer. Add the wrong or minor arrangements you want to explain; anything not listed gets the general message below.

Authoring prototype. Off by default: a step asks a stereo question only when the author turns it on. The right answer is the product as drawn on the Mechanism stage (change it there). “Either enantiomer counts as right” decides whether the mirror image of the answer (and of each alternative) counts the same; it starts from the state's racemic flag and warns when the two disagree. Each alternative is built with the same tap control the learner uses, then given a verdict (wrong, or minor product) and a message; live checks flag a duplicate, a copy of the right answer (or of its mirror image when the product is racemic), and a missing message. “Try it as a learner” runs the learner's control with what you wrote.
