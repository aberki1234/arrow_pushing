Prototype C, second round. New stereocentres (or a new C=C) in the product start as “?”. Tap one, or Tab to it and press Enter, to draw it: first a wedge, then a dash (a C=C flips between its two geometries). “Check product” unlocks once every “?” is set. A wrong or minor arrangement counts as a wrong try and says why; you then change it and check again. For a racemic product the mirror image counts too (hydroboration). Scenes: SN2 also offers “racemic mixture”; bromination step 2 has one new centre next to one already set; hydroboration sets two at once; Felkin–Anh has a minor diastereomer (“plausible”); E2 is an E/Z choice.
