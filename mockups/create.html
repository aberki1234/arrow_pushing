    Mockup notes: what changes from the current editor

    • A Create tab on the learner site lists your mechanisms with their status; the editor opens from there.
    • Four stages, one job each. Stage 1 asks only for a name and the molecules (one SMILES box or a sketcher); stage 2 only for arrows; stage 3 is a to-do list of teaching text, one item open at a time; stage 4 holds the library details, checks and submit.
    • Most fields are hidden until they matter. Show H, change steps, wedges and abbreviated groups sit under "⋯ More". Step keys, state ids, partial arrow sets, spectators, annotations and atom roles sit under "Advanced". Difficulty is suggested.
    • Lint becomes the to-do list. Errors read as tasks with a "Fix →" link, not a panel of rule ids.
    • Stages are a suggested order, not locks: you can jump back to stage 2 at any time.
