Start · Hints

Hint 2: The C–Br carbon is δ+. Carbon is bonded to bromine, which pulls electrons away: carbon is δ+. Where must its electrons go?

Start · Common wrong answers +1

New: lone pair on Br → C

Message: Bromide is the leaving group: it leaves with the electrons rather than attacking the carbon it is leaving.

Severity: wrong

Start → Product · Why it happens

Hydroxide attacks the δ+ carbon from the back as bromide leaves, so the configuration inverts (Walden inversion).

Mockup notes: proposals still to agree

  • Notifications in the site only for now: a count on Review for reviewers, a banner on Your mechanisms for contributors. Email (e.g. via Resend) can come later.
  • Limits: at most 5 open drafts per contributor; anything more waits until one is submitted or deleted. Blocking a user (Users page) stops their saving and submitting.
  • Submitted drafts stay editable, but editing takes them out of review (back to draft) so the reviewer never reviews a moving target.
  • Review notes: one overall note plus optional notes pinned to a change; they show on the contributor's Publish stage with "Go there →", and clear when they submit again.
  • The diff lists only what changed by default: text shown with deletions and insertions, a changed state as before/after drawings, a new wrong answer as its drawing. A new reaction shows everything.
  • A reviewer can't review their own submission; the owner can publish their own reactions directly, as now.
