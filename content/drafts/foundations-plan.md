# Foundations F1–F5: reaction plan and drafting brief

Plan for the first five Foundations tracks of `docs/curriculum.md` (2026-11-26). Each track is drafted into its own
folder, `content/drafts/F<n>-<track-slug>/`, with its track file in that folder as `track.json`. Nothing here is
pushed to Create until the owner has reviewed the plan's output.

## The reactions

Slugs are the reaction slugs. "(published)" reuses a reaction already on the site; it is listed in the track file but
not drafted again. Each track ends in a checkpoint that reuses 4–6 of its own reactions.

### F1 Curved arrows — `curved-arrows`

1. **Sources and sinks**
   - `hydroxide-and-hydronium`: HO⁻ + H₃O⁺ → 2 H₂O (one step: lone pair → H, O–H σ → O).
   - `ammonia-and-boron-trifluoride`: NH₃ + BF₃ → H₃N⁺–BF₃⁻ (one arrow).
   - `chloride-and-tert-butyl-cation`: Cl⁻ + (CH₃)₃C⁺ → t-BuCl (one arrow).
   - `water-and-tert-butyl-cation`: H₂O + (CH₃)₃C⁺ → t-BuOH₂⁺ (one arrow), then deprotonation by a second water.
2. **Formal charge and octets**
   - `protonating-water-with-hcl`: H₂O + HCl → H₃O⁺ + Cl⁻.
   - `protonating-ammonia`: NH₃ + H₃O⁺ → NH₄⁺ + H₂O.
   - `tert-butyloxonium-loses-water`: t-BuOH₂⁺ → t-Bu⁺ + H₂O (one arrow; feedback on a pentavalent carbon).
3. **Resonance** (each one or two resonance steps, the second form drawn as the to-state)
   - `acetate-resonance`, `acetone-enolate-resonance`, `allyl-cation-resonance`, `amide-resonance`,
     `nitromethane-resonance`.
4. **Proton transfer and pKa**
   - `acetic-acid-and-hydroxide`, `phenol-and-hydroxide` (phenoxide resonance explained),
     `protonating-acetone` (H₃O⁺; the oxocarbenium resonance form), `ethoxide-and-water` (which way it goes:
     misconception for the reverse direction).
5. **Checkpoint**: five reactions from sets 1–4.

### F2 Nucleophilic substitution — `nucleophilic-substitution`

1. **SN2**: `sn2-hydroxide-bromomethane` (published), `cyanide-and-iodoethane`, `finkelstein-chloroethane`
   (I⁻, acetone), `azide-and-benzyl-bromide`, `ammonia-and-iodomethane` (SN2 then deprotonation).
2. **SN1**: `tert-butyl-bromide-hydrolysis` (ionisation, water attacks, deprotonation),
   `benzhydryl-chloride-methanolysis`, `allylic-chloride-hydrolysis` (3-chlorobut-1-ene + water: the allylic cation,
   the two regioisomeric alcohols as main and alternative products), `3-methylbutan-2-ol-and-hbr` (protonation,
   loss of water, hydride shift, bromide).
3. **Making leaving groups**: `ethanol-and-hbr` (protonate, then SN2), `tosylate-formation` (TsCl, pyridine),
   `tosylate-displacement` (iodide on an alkyl tosylate), `appel-reaction` (PPh₃, CBr₄, an alcohol).
4. **Checkpoint**.

### F3 Elimination — `elimination`

1. **E2**: `e2-2-bromopropane-ethoxide`, `e2-2-bromobutane-zaitsev` (but-2-ene preferred, but-1-ene accepted as an
   alternative product), `e2-tert-amyl-bromide-hofmann` (t-BuOK: 2-methylbut-1-ene preferred, 2-methylbut-2-ene
   accepted).
2. **E1**: `e1-tert-butanol-dehydration` (H₂SO₄: protonate, lose water, deprotonate),
   `e1-dehydration-with-methyl-shift` (3,3-dimethylbutan-2-ol: a 1,2-methyl shift before deprotonation).
3. **E1cB**: `e1cb-diacetone-alcohol` (NaOH: enolate, then hydroxide leaves → mesityl oxide).
4. **Substitution or elimination?**: `1-bromopropane-and-ethoxide` (SN2), `tert-butyl-bromide-and-ethoxide` (E2),
   `1-bromopropane-and-tert-butoxide` (E2). Misconceptions point out the other pathway.
5. **Checkpoint**.

### F4 Addition to alkenes — `addition-to-alkenes`

1. **Via carbocations**: `propene-and-hbr` (Markovnikov; the anti-Markovnikov attack as a misconception),
   `2-methylpropene-hydration` (H₃O⁺: protonate, water adds, deprotonate), `styrene-and-hbr` (benzylic cation),
   `3-3-dimethylbut-1-ene-and-hcl` (methyl shift before chloride adds).
2. **Via three-membered rings**: `cyclohexene-bromination` (bromonium, then bromide opens it anti),
   `2-methylpropene-bromohydrin` (Br₂/H₂O: water at the more substituted carbon), `cyclohexene-epoxidation`
   (mCPBA, one concerted step).
3. **Hydroboration**: `propene-hydroboration` (BH₃, concerted four-centre step), `alkylborane-oxidation`
   (H₂O₂/NaOH: hydroperoxide adds to B, 1,2-alkyl shift B→O, hydrolysis).
4. **Checkpoint**.

### F5 Carbonyl addition — `carbonyl-addition`

1. **Nucleophilic addition**: `sodium-borohydride-and-acetone` (hydride from B–H, then protonation by methanol),
   `methylmagnesium-bromide-and-benzaldehyde` (addition, then an acid work-up as a change step and protonation),
   `cyanohydrin-formation` (CN⁻ adds, HCN protonates and regenerates CN⁻).
2. **Hydrates, hemiacetals and acetals**: `acetaldehyde-hydrate` (base-catalysed),
   `cyclic-hemiacetal-formation` (5-hydroxypentanal, acid), `benzaldehyde-dimethyl-acetal` (MeOH, H⁺: the full
   sequence through the oxocarbenium ion).
3. **Imines and enamines**: `acetone-methylamine-imine` (acid catalysis), `cyclohexanone-pyrrolidine-enamine`,
   `reductive-amination-iminium-reduction` (NaBH₃CN reduces the iminium ion).
4. **Ylides**: `phosphonium-ylide-formation` (Ph₃P⁺CH₃ Br⁻ + BuLi), `wittig-methylenation-cyclohexanone`
   (published).
5. **Checkpoint**.

## The brief for whoever drafts a track

Read first: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/schema-design.md` §2–§5 (state SMILES, the arrow notation, the lint rules, what
counts as correct), `schema/reaction.schema.json`, one example reaction in `fixtures/reactions/`, and two published
SEAr reactions in `content/published/` (e.g. `nitration-of-benzene/1.json`, `nitration-of-toluene/1.json`).

**Files.** One reaction file per reaction in the track's folder, `<reactionSlug>.json`, version 1,
`previousReactionVersion` null, `authors` `[{"name": "Maksim", "url": null}]`, `licence` `CC-BY-SA-4.0`,
`references` Clayden, Greeves and Warren, *Organic Chemistry*, 2nd edition (OUP), with the chapter. The track file is
`track.json` in the same folder (shape: `schema/track.schema.json`; example: `content/tracks/
electrophilic-aromatic-substitution.json`): slug as above, problem sets in the plan's order, a concept card per set
(the small Markdown subset: paragraphs, **bold**, *italic*, lists), a `whyThisOne` for every item, and a checkpoint
reusing 4–6 of the track's reactions. Writing the files from a small Python script of your own (kept outside the
repository) is the quickest way to keep SMILES and arrows consistent.

**Structures.** Fully mapped Kekulé SMILES, every heavy atom bracketed and mapped; a hydrogen is an explicit mapped
atom `[H:n]` only if some step's arrows touch it. Map numbers stay with their atom in every state. Counter-ions and
reagents are real species (no bare H⁺, no unnamed "base"). Reagents that arrive later (work-up) are change steps with
`addedSpecies`.

**Arrows.** The notation of `docs/schema-design.md` §2 (`lp O1 -> σ O1-C2`, `π C1-C2 -> π C2-C3`,
`σ C2-Br3 -> lp Br3`). Every step's canonical arrows must give its to-state exactly. One preferred route; other correct
routes are `accepted`; a regioisomer may end at an alternative product with a caption.

**Learner text.** Every state with an arrow step leaving it has a `learnerPrompt` and three `hintTiers`
(conceptual → narrowing → the answer in words). **Learners never see map numbers: never write "C13" or "O4" in
learner-facing text.** Name atoms by role ("the carbonyl carbon", "the oxygen of water", "the bromine"). Add 1–3
`misconceptions` per state for the common wrong answers (arrow-pattern grammar in `docs/schema-design.md` §5.2;
patterns may use map numbers, they are not shown) and `partialProgressMessages` for an obvious incomplete answer.
`explanationAfterStep` on every step, `caption` on every state, `mechanismSummary`, a short `subtitle`, a
`conditionsLabel` with real textbook conditions, `difficultyTier` 1–3, `dailyPuzzleEligible` (true only if some step
forms or breaks a σ bond other than an acidic O/N/S/halogen–H bond; resonance drills and plain proton transfers are
false: owner, 2026-11-26),
`reactionClasses` from the schema's list. British spelling, plain words, short sentences.

**Checks, all must pass before you report:** from the repository root, with
`export PATH=$HOME/miniconda3/envs/mech_training/bin:$PATH`:
- `npx tsx src/reactionLinting/runFromShell.ts seed-coordinates <files>` (lays out each state; molecules kept apart),
- `npx tsx src/reactionLinting/runFromShell.ts lint <files>`: 0 errors, and no warnings you cannot justify,
- `npx tsx /tmp/claude-1000/-home-u6938219-mech-training/81adec37-d5f6-4ece-a10a-0decb0bf84d7/scratchpad/check_drawable.ts <files>`:
  every arrow can be drawn with the app's picking,
- the track file: copy your reaction files and the published ones it uses (`content/published/<slug>/1.json`) into a
  temporary folder outside the repository and run `npx tsx src/reactionLinting/runFromShell.ts lint-tracks
  <folder>/track.json --published-folder <that folder>`.

Do not change anything outside the track's folder, do not run `make test` or Vitest, do not commit, push or run
`push-draft`. If a reaction in the plan cannot be represented (an unsupported element, a step the linter refuses that
is chemically right), leave it out, say why, and suggest a replacement in your report.

**Reaction classes** (`reactionClasses`, schema enum): `acid-base` (added for F1: proton transfers, Lewis adducts,
resonance drills), `substitution`, `aromatic substitution`, `addition` (added for F4), `elimination`, `carbonyl`,
`pericyclic`, `rearrangement`, `radical`. A reaction may have more than one (e.g. a carbocation addition with a
methyl shift: `addition`, `rearrangement`).
